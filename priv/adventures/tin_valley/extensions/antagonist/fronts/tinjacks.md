Rusk, a deserter from the levy, with his brute Cobb and the boy Pip as lookout. They call themselves the Tinjacks and hide in the old adit off the west road. They act on world time whether or not the player engages: a robbery rumour, then a break-in at the Guild's weighing shed, then a "road toll" at the Valley Inn, then a burning.
