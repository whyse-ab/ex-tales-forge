# Cobb

Rusk's muscle, a former quarryman.

## Appearance

Huge, slow-moving, a knotted club and a broken nose set crooked.

## Personality

Few words, quick temper, loyal to Rusk like a dog. Likes the toll because people are afraid of him.

## Backstory

Followed Rusk out of the levy because Rusk always knew what to do next.

## Motivations

Primary need: belonging. Current concern: doing what Rusk says.
