# Rusk

A deserter from the duke's levy who leads the Tinjacks, a gang of three that robs the west road.

## Appearance

Lean, weathered, a levy coat with the badge cut off, a long knife, and an easy smile that never reaches his eyes.

## Personality

Cold, patient, reasonable-sounding. Prefers a deal to a fight and a threat to a deal. Backs off the moment the odds turn.

## Backstory

Walked away from a losing war with two friends' pay. Found the old adit, found that the valley blames the orcs for everything, and found that fear pays.

## Motivations

Primary need: coin and safety. Current concern: squeezing the village before the Guild clears the cut and looks around.
