# Pip

Fifteen, the Tinjacks' lookout on the west road, and Old Maude's grandson.

## Appearance

Thin, freckled, a whistle on a string, boots too good for him (stolen).

## Personality

Cocky in front of Rusk, scared when alone, ashamed when his grandmother is mentioned.

## Backstory

Ran off with Rusk for the coin and the swagger. Now he knows about the clubbed watchman and wants out, but fears what Rusk would do.

## Motivations

Primary need: safety. Current concern: getting out of the gang alive.
