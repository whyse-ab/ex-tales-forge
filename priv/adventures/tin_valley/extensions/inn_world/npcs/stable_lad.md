# Tam Holt

Brenna's nephew, fourteen, who keeps the inn's stable and yard.

## Appearance

Straw in his hair, a too-big drover's coat, quick eyes that never stop moving.

## Personality

Nosy, cheeky, fast on his feet. Proud of knowing every back way in the village and every face that passes the yard at night. Brags, then goes quiet when a grown-up scares him.

## Backstory

His father died in the shaft two winters ago; Brenna took him in. He sleeps above the stable and hears the drovers talk.

## Motivations

Primary need: to be taken seriously. Current concern: the night carts on the west road and the men who drive them.
