# Old Maude

The village herb-wife and healer, older than anyone can remember, and the valley's memory.

## Appearance

Small and bent, quick hands stained green, a shawl full of pockets, eyes that miss nothing.

## Personality

Dry, kind, unhurried. Tells stories sideways and expects you to work out the point. Fierce about the people she has stitched up.

## Backstory

She buried two husbands from the shaft and nursed half the village. She remembers why the old workings were closed.

## Motivations

Primary need: to keep her people whole. Current concern: the Guild digging deeper, and the young ones going wild.
