# Mags Ferrow

A drover who hauls salt, rope and lamp oil up the valley road and tin back down.

## Appearance

Broad, sunburnt, a mule whip coiled at her belt and a ledger stuffed in her coat.

## Personality

Loud, practical, haggles for sport and pays her debts. Furious about her lost cart and done with being told it was orcs.

## Backstory

Twenty years on the valley road. Last week a cart of salt vanished on the west road while she slept at the inn.

## Motivations

Primary need: her livelihood. Current concern: getting her salt back, or the coin for it.
