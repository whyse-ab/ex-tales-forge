# Hilde Brask

The village smith. She points the Guild's picks and makes the only decent blades in the valley.

## Appearance

Forearms scarred by sparks, a leather apron, grey hair cropped short against the heat.

## Personality

Blunt, proud of her work, short with fools and soft on apprentices. Lately uneasy, and quick to change the subject.

## Backstory

Took the forge from her father. The Guild pays late, so she has been buying cheap tin from men who come to her back door after dark, and has not asked where it comes from.

## Motivations

Primary need: to keep the forge going. Current concern: the cheap tin, and what it may cost her.
