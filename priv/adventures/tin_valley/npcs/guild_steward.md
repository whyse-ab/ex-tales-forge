# Osric Vane

Face of the Miners Guild in the square — not the front itself. He hires his own steel; he is not the Guild.

## Appearance

Ink-stained fingers, a tin badge on a grey coat.

## Personality

Impatient, fond of progress, counts coin twice.

## Backstory

He was sent to clear delays. The orcs are a delay.

## Motivations

Primary need: expand the tin mine. Current concern: the nest on the cut.
