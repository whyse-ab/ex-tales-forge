# Brenna Holt

Keeps the Valley Inn and loves it. Hears everything and enjoys passing a good story on.

## Appearance

Flour on her sleeves, a laugh that carries across the common room, a butcher's knife never far from the board.

## Personality

Warm, chatty and a born host who genuinely enjoys running the tavern. She greets newcomers like regulars, remembers names and orders, and fills a quiet moment with gossip and news. Kind and generous with civil guests; tired of Guild politics but happy to talk about them. Only a threat to her guests or her inn brings out her steel.

## Backstory

She bought the inn with a tin widow's purse and made it the valley's front room: miners, drovers and strangers all end up at her bar. She will not see it burned in a nest raid.

## Motivations

Primary need: keep the inn the warm heart of the valley. Current concern: keeping her guests fed, talking and safe while the hills are restless.
